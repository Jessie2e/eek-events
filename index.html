EEK Castleton · M.S. Event Management, NYU Tisch School of Hospitality

          
EEK Castleton is a creative producer with 15 years’ experience in event management for weddings and in the nonprofit and performing arts sectors. They have designed, executed and marketed events ranging from touring circus shows to site-specific performances; large-scale conferences to art exhibitions; and golf tournaments to live-streamed panels, cocktail parties and galas.

          
They have consulted on and worked weddings as intimate as an Adirondack cabin elopement, as exotic as a dance party on a Hawaiian island, as unpretentious as a potluck on a Massachusetts farm, and as Instagram-perfect as a steamboat cruise under the stars.

          
While at Girl Scouts of the USA, EEK led production for the Alum team’s popular Campfire Chat series — 22 live virtual events that drove more than 50,000 registrations. They have worked in close partnership with corporate social responsibility departments to engage participants and meet objectives. During their tenure with Big Apple Circus, EEK produced member and cultivation events in three states.

          
From facilitating intergenerational family reunions for recreation organizations to promoting national tours of NYT Critic’s Pick Off-Broadway circus shows and fireside chats with CEOs of Fortune 500 companies, EEK’s experience in event management and promotions is a juggling act. They use exemplary relationship-building skills to work cross-functionally across the full event cycle, from brainstorming to reporting — collaborating internally with marketing, communications, fund development, legal, finance, the Offices of the Board and CEO, and externally with vendors, speakers, brand partners and consultants.
