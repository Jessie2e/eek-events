# EEK Events — evolved web style direction

## Core idea
**Modern editorial fantasy.** Not “Renaissance Faire website,” not dark-academia cosplay, and not generic luxury wedding beige. The brand should feel like a fashion/editorial studio that also happens to understand tabletop campaigns, costume design, lore, immersive worlds, queer joy, accessibility, and people who commit to the bit.

The emotional promise is: **You do not have to sand off the parts of yourselves that make the wedding yours.**

## Design thesis
The existing identity already has a strong foundation: medieval/editorial type, forest + oxblood + copper + sage, textured imagery, and “Main Character Energy.” Keep those. Then modernize the system with sharp layouts, oversized typography, clean sans-serif UI copy, playful interaction, and one unexpected digital accent.

### Palette
- Forest — `#343B29` (existing core)
- Oxblood — `#55212A` (existing core)
- Copper — `#A46136` (existing core)
- Sage — `#ADB0A9` (existing core)
- Parchment — `#F3F0E7` (new neutral for readable editorial space)
- Ink — `#12130F` (new near-black)
- Portal Violet — `#8B7CFF` (new digital/fantasy accent; use sparingly)
- Critical-Hit Acid — `#D7FF70` (new micro-accent for CTAs, dice, hover states)

## Type
- Display: **IM Fell English** — preserve the existing EEK character.
- UI/body: **Space Grotesk** — makes the site feel contemporary and keeps longer copy readable.

## Visual vocabulary
Use: oversized type, editorial cropping, circular “portal” lines, thin borders, campaign/case-file language, subtle dice references, scroll-snap storytelling, asymmetrical photo grids, custom microcopy, parchment/ink contrast, and surprising hover states.

Avoid: literal dragons everywhere, fake parchment backgrounds on every section, gothic blackletter body copy, “geek wedding” clip art, excessive D&D terminology, or anything that makes the couple feel like they need to belong to a fandom to hire EEK.

## Conversion strategy
The site should make the right couple think **“oh, they get us”** within five seconds, then make inquiry effortless. The homepage therefore moves through:

1. Identity hook — Main Character Energy.
2. Proof — real EEK images + “too weird works.”
3. Portfolio — named couple case files.
4. Services — package names become an elegant campaign system.
5. Vibe Forge — playful lead qualification and concept generator.
6. Founder/team trust.
7. Strong direct inquiry CTA.

## Micro-interactions in the prototype
- Animated top marquee.
- Circular portal animation over the hero image.
- “Roll for vibes” d20 with randomized wedding prophecy.
- Scroll reveal transitions.
- Horizontal case-study carousel.
- Hover motion on package cards and photography.
- Interactive Wedding Vibe Forge that recommends a package and generates an inquiry brief.
- Hidden easter egg: click the EEK logo five times quickly.
- Reduced-motion support for accessibility.
